# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — Initial public release

- Home Assistant history and live inputs with normal configuration panels.
- CNN load forecasting with weekly context, calendar and temperature features, age weighting and guarded model updates.
- Independent LightGBM solar models for different panel orientations and a fading near-term production correction.
- Battery efficiency calibration and projections that account for inverter limits, reserve and tariff windows.
- Separate stored-energy excess and achievable peak export, with selectable projection lines and synchronized chart tooltips.
- Issued-forecast measurement, archived-weather backtests, weather-model comparison and a direct CNN trial.
- Read-only reporting through the separate Energy Excess HACS integration.
