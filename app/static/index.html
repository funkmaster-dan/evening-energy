Choosing history and handling gaps

For seasonal patterns, use a year or more of history and keep 28–56 recent days for validation. Battery calibration uses 5-minute readings. Consumption gaps stay empty; missing solar readings during full darkness are treated as zero. A unit override is only needed when recorded units are unavailable.

How scenarios are calculated

Conservative uses lower solar and higher consumption (P10/P90); middle uses P50/P50; optimistic uses P90/P10. Energy totals are estimated for 06:00–18:00 and 18:00–06:00 local time, following the hourly forecast shape.

More uncertainty samples reduce sampling variation. The battery shading compares all three scenarios at the selected export limit; it is not a probability interval.
